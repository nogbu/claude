# Review checklist

Kinds of mistakes the reviewer checks every draft for. Each one is a kind of mistake, not a single case. The example in brackets is where it came from.

When Nwani catches a mistake the reviewer missed, add its kind here. Don't add the single example.

1. **Comparing things that aren't comparable.** Both sides must be the same kind of thing, equally complete, and from the same point in time. If they aren't, the comparison can't be made yet, or only its fair part can. [A full fantasy team was ranked against half-drafted ones, 2026-10-19.]
2. **Saying something exists or doesn't without looking.** A rule, file, setting, fact or option. Search first. [Said there was no rule when there was one.]
3. **Stating a fact a source could confirm, without checking the source.** [Gave a fertilizer dosage that was wrong while the right one sat in a learnings file.]
4. **Answering the literal question instead of the goal behind it.** [Ranked teams because that was asked, when the point was knowing where the team really stood.]
5. **Presenting a partial result as complete.** If the answer rests on partial data, a sample or a cut-off read, say so up front. [Read only the first 250 characters of search results.]
6. **More confidence than the evidence supports.** Guesses and inferences are labeled as such.
7. **Missing the basic, well-known options.** Before recommending, check that the standard approaches were considered. [Dozens of speed ideas, none of them running tasks in parallel.]
8. **Treating silence or a change of subject as a yes.** Only Nwani's answer counts as approval. [Saved a change after the subject changed.]
9. **Narrowing a general problem to the example given.** When Nwani describes a problem with an example, the fix must cover the whole kind of problem. [Built a fix for data comparisons when the problem was every kind of answer, 2026-10-20.]
10. **A list that skips whole kinds of what was asked for.** Before calling a list done, name the main kinds the answer should cover and check each one. [Listed stores that carry a brand, mostly department stores, and left out most online-only, flash-sale and resale stores, 2026-10-22.]
