equipment
Assume these unless I say otherwise.

- Paper printer: Brother HL-L2390DW (Amazon ASIN B0764NWFP9). Monochrome laser: prints black only, so colors come out as shades of gray. Max resolution 2400 × 600 dpi. 4.2 mm unprintable margin on every side. Automatic duplex. 250-sheet tray plus a 1-sheet manual feed slot. Paper: Letter, Legal, A4 and custom sizes 3.0–8.5 in wide × 5.0–14 in long. Wi-Fi, Wi-Fi Direct and USB. Its flatbed scanner isn't available for measuring.
- Phone: Pixel 10 Pro.
