# lecaaddict

Content file for the Plantagram project (added 2026-10-02). LECA Addict's semi-hydro guidance. LECA Addict is a favored source (added 2026-10-01).

## Learnings (read 2026-10-01)

- **Transitions:** RapidStart from 2022 onward (earlier, seaweed + SUPERthrive). Stimulants once a month at 1–2 mL/gal, skipped at other feedings.  
- **Probiotics** with every watering.  
- **Rotting roots during transitions:** Physan 20 sprayed at the root base, or a shower with H₂O₂ added.  
- **CalMag first** when using filtered water.  
- **pH:** about 5.7–5.8, or skip adjusting (citing First Rays).  
- **Feeding strength:** ¼–½ strength when starting out.  
- **Silica:** dropped after seeing little difference.
