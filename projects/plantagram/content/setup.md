# setup

Content file for the Plantagram project. My grow setup (added 2026-09-25).

## Collection

- Mostly **anthuriums**, plus a couple of **variegated monsteras** and some **philodendrons**.

## Cabinets

- Three greenhouse cabinets: [UNICOLY 4-tier glass display cabinet](https://www.amazon.com/UNICOLY-Glass-Display-Cabinet-4-Tier/dp/B0DX1GKH3F). Measured dimensions are in unicoly (projects/3dp/content/unicoly.md).  
- One of the shelves is 17 inches high.  
- Average conditions: **78–80°F** and **75–80% humidity**.

## Lighting

- Two [Barrina T5 LED grow lights](https://www.barrina-led.com/products/barrina-t5-10w-led-grow-lights-2ft-5000k-full-spectrum-magnetic-linkable-nf10) per shelf level: model NF10, 2 ft, 10 W each (20 W per level), 5000K full spectrum, magnetic and linkable. Barrina doesn't publish PPFD figures.  
- On from 9 am to 11 pm every day (**14 hours**).

## Air circulation

- **Two fans per cabinet, on alternating shelf levels** (6 fans total across the three cabinets): [be quiet! Silent Wings Pro 4 140mm PWM](https://www.bequiet.com/en/casefans/3706), with a medium/high/ultra-high speed switch. (Previously four per cabinet, one per level; reduced 2026-09-25 to cut reservoir evaporation.)  
- Fans run 24/7 at **full speed (ultra-high)**. I can't change the speed.  
- Speed switch specs: ultra-high 2,400 rpm, 97.4 CFM, 36.8 dB(A), 3.64 mm H₂O static pressure (be quiet! product page). Medium about 1,100 rpm and high about 1,900 rpm (from an [Enostech review](https://www.enostech.com/silent-wings-4-pro-120-140mm-fans-review/), not an official spec). be quiet! doesn't publish airflow for medium or high; *(inference)* scaling by rpm gives roughly 45 CFM and 77 CFM.

## Growing method

- **Semi-hydroponics** in self-watering pots that use the **immersion method** (**no wick**).  
- Pot sizes: 3", 3.5", 4.5", and 6.5".  
- **Pots:** closed-bottom pots with side slots at the waterline, so the reservoir is the bottom of the pot itself, under an insert; there's no separate reservoir to lift out. Current pots are the older (legacy) 3, 3.5, 4.5 and 6.5 in pots; their design and the new family's are in nursery-pots (3dp project, projects/3dp/products/nursery-pots/state.md). Each sits in a decorative outer pot that catches overflow; none are watertight (2026-10-03).  
- **Watered every 7 days.**

## Substrates

- **DIY pon:** 9 parts pumice : 1 part zeolite. Used **only for juvenile and mature plants**.  
- **Pron:** 1 part pumice : 1 part Fluval Stratum. Used **only for seedlings, props (cuttings/propagations), deflasked tissue culture, and plants being rehabbed**.

## Water (added 2026-09-25)

- Base water: **tap water run through a ZeroWater filter.**  
- *(inference)* ZeroWater's ion-exchange stage strips nearly all dissolved minerals, so treat it like RO/distilled water: **Ca and Mg must be supplemented**, and it has almost no buffering, so solution pH can swing more than with tap water.

## Tools (added 2026-09-26)

- **Photone** light meter app with the **official physical diffuser**, for measuring PPFD.
