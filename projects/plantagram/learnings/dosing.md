# learnings/dosing

Learned in the flutes thread (2026-10-08).

- **TPS's stated rate for the use comes first** (2026-10-08, at my request): take the dose TPS gives for that exact use (product page or FAQ, feedchart row, label) before deriving one. Claude proposed Signal at 4 mL from "half the weekly label rate" when TPS One's FAQ states 2 mL/gal in bloom; I corrected it.  
- **Then check the running EC against a target** (2026-10-08): nutes set TPS One at its label minimum and checked the measured running EC (\~930) against the Dutch 800–1,200 range. Size the flexible dose (usually TPS One) to the target from each step's measured µS/cm per mL, and show the arithmetic.  
- **Weigh the setup and the other recipes before proposing** (2026-10-08, I asked): light (65–85 PPFD), warm root zone (78–80°F), fans concentrating reservoirs, base water needing CalMag, zeolite buffering, nutes' day-7 runoff table, putes' salt-stress and root-rot findings.  
- **TPS feedcharts** (read 2026-10-08): every chart is a week-by-week cannabis schedule; none covers houseplants or anthuriums. Summary in inventory (TPS Nutrients website sections); read it there instead of refetching.
