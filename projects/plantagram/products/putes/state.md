# putes

Plantagram product doc (created 2026-10-01).

## Current version

- **v9, 2026-10-08:** written per ½ gallon, the batch I mix (v8 was per gallon).  
- **Scope:** pron plants, only the first two weeks of waterings after a repot or root rot (day 0 and day 7). Also the refill at the end of nutes' monthly flush, using the day-7 batch (agreed 2026-10-08).

## Recipe (per ½ gallon of base water)

Add one product at a time, stirring between; never combine concentrates.

| 1 | Base water | ½ gal | \~0 *(measured)* | No minerals; Ca and Mg come from the mix |
| 2 | API Tap Water Conditioner | 2 drops | \~0 *(measured at 4 drops)* | Above API's chloramine label rate of 3 drops/gal (2026-10-02, at my request); protects the microbes if the filter misses any |

| 8 | TPS pH Up | 6 drops | — | My dose (agreed 2026-10-09). Target 5.5–5.8: low end of TPS's 5.5–6.2, since Pythium is less common below 5.5 (PT Horticulture) *(inference)* |

Basis and Decisions give rates per gallon, as sources do.

## Use

1. **Mix fresh each watering**; nothing keeps once mixed. Day 7 skips RapidStart (stimulants about a month apart; First Rays, via LECA Addict) but keeps Kelpak per First Rays' repot directions, though the maker says no closer than 10 days apart (see inventory).
2. **Day 0:** first rinse any H₂O₂ or Physan 20 off the roots with base water (both kill Root Boost's microbes); water in from the top and fill the reservoir.
3. **Mid-week top-ups:** plain base water (Foliage Factory, as evaporation pushes reservoir EC up).
4. **Day 7, before flushing:** measure and log runoff EC and pH. If pH is under 5.0, set the day-7 batch near 6.0 *(inference; Foliage Factory: K, Ca, Mg and P become less available below 5.0)*.
5. **In pon:** same recipe, but judge by new roots more than day-7 runoff, since zeolite holds and releases salts *(inference)*.
6. **pH:** base water barely buffers; after the 6 drops of pH Up, stir, check after a minute, and if outside 5.5–5.8 adjust with pH Down or Up in tiny steps (as in nutes).
7. **Kelpak:** measure and log EC and pH after adding it on the first batch (neither known yet). Refrigerate once opened (First Rays: fine); use within 3 weeks (maker).

## Day 0 root-rot prep (added 2026-10-02)

**Physan soak (per ½ gallon of base water):** mix enough to cover the roots, crown and stem base; if ½ gal won't, double every rate for 1 gal. Measure the Physan (Physan: never by eye, never above this rate). No CalMag (sanitizing soak; Dosing rule, 2026-10-09).

| Order | Product | Rate per ½ gallon | EC after adding (µS/cm) | Basis |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Base water | ½ gal | \~0 *(measured)* | As in the recipe |
| 2 | API Tap Water Conditioner | 2 drops | \~0 *(measured at 4 drops)* | Dosing rule (2026-10-02) |
| 3 | Physan 20 | 7.4 mL | Not measured | Physan's orchid rot and dividing rate, 1 Tbsp/gal; mL conversion *(inference)* |

1. Unpot, wash off all old substrate in base water, discard the old pon *(inference)*.
2. Cut every brown, mushy or hollow root back to firm tissue, keeping the blade wet with the soak (Physan).
3. Dip the roots, crown and stem base in the solution, then spray both sides of every leaf and soak for 10 minutes (agreed 2026-10-09). On velvet anthuriums, rinse leaves with base water after *(inference)*.
4. Rinse roots thoroughly in base water (Use, step 2).
5. Repot into fresh pron in a new pot: fill about ⅓, spread the roots, add pron a little at a time while shaking the pot (First Rays).
6. Water in with putes (Use, step 2).

**Aftercare, days 1–14** *(inference unless noted)*: shelf without a fan (airflow lowers the dry line and evaporation cools roots, per First Rays); velvet anthuriums: also check reservoir pH mid-week; separate or sanitized tools; check tray neighbors' roots; no bag or heat mat (cabinets run 78–80°F, 75–80% humidity; setup). Day 14: look for firm new root tips; repeat the prep if roots still rot. Keep stress low 4–6 weeks in all (First Rays).

## Decisions and why

- **Built from scratch (2026-10-01, at my request):** every inventory product and dose rederived from sources, ignoring nutes' doses and exclusions.
- **Low salt:** brief salt stress sharply raises water-mold root rot: MacDonald 1982 (chrysanthemum), 70–88% of salt-stressed roots lesioned vs. 20% unstressed, at far more salt than any feed; a 2025 hydroponic study found Pythium rot worse under salinity. Foliage Factory: recovery phases under 400 µS/cm.
- **Little nitrogen, not none (sources disagree):** First Rays: don't fertilize until established; Dip 'N Grow's FAQ: rooting media gain nothing from fertilizer before roots form. A tomato–Pythium study found no N worse than moderate, and excess N weakens defenses (PT Horticulture). So N comes only with calcium and microbes. Fluval: Stratum carries its own nutrients.
- **Microbes, not sanitizers:** Sutton et al. 2006 rank root-zone microbes, oxygenation and solution temperature among the best levers against hydroponic Pythium. LECA Addict differs (in transitions, Physan 20 sprayed at the root base or H₂O₂ showers for rotting roots, plus probiotics every watering), as does TPS (Root Boost's root-rot dose with 7 mL/gal of 3% H₂O₂ in hydro reservoirs); HygroZyme caps H₂O₂ at 2 mL/gal (re-read 2026-10-02).
- **Root Boost at 6 mL/gal** (up from 5, 2026-10-02, at my request): top of TPS's root-rot range.
- **RapidStart at 2 mL/gal, day 0 only** (added 2026-10-01, at my request): LECA Addict, a favored semi-hydro source, uses it in transitions; that outranks its earlier exclusion as unproven.
- **Kelpak at 15 mL/gal, day 0 and 7** (added 2026-10-02, at my request): the rooting stimulant; chosen over Dyna-Gro K-L-N (IBA + NAA) and Rhizopon AA tablets (IBA): gentler (no reported flower deformities; maker: overdosing unlikely), both favored semi-hydro sources use it, and independent studies show it aids rooting; synthetic IBA has deeper rooting research. 1:500 or weaker is ineffective (First Rays). First Rays' timing: water in, then the next two waterings. RapidStart stays on day 0: it isn't a hormone, so stacking shouldn't overstimulate *(inference)*.
- **CalMag at 2 mL/gal** (up from 1.5, 2026-10-01, at my request): more Ca for new root tips, Mg further inside First Rays' range, and a measured EC for the step. Trade-off: final EC (Recipe) is above the 400 recovery band (Low salt); N rises about 7 ppm (inference; Claude's math).
- **Physan soak at repot** (added 2026-10-02, at my request): from Physan's orchid rot directions (soak, trim, soak the wounds), simplified to trim, then one 10-minute dip with leaf spray (agreed 2026-10-09). Rinsed off before putes to spare the microbes, though Physan says not to rinse and to pot into Physan-dampened medium. Dips are timed: a grower reported limp plants after an 8-hour soak. Rate (verified on Physan's pages 2026-10-09): 1 Tbsp/gal is its orchid rot soak; its orchid leaf sprays are 1–2 tsp/gal, but one mix at 1 Tbsp/gal covers the leaves too (agreed 2026-10-09).
- **Left out** (detail in log: Left-out products):
  - H₂O₂ and Physan 20 in the solution: they kill the microbes.
  - TPS One: a full fertilizer (see nitrogen).
  - Silica Gold: mixed evidence against Pythium; raises pH in unbuffered water; needs changing every 4–5 days (TPS).
  - Dip 'N Grow: made for cuttings; diluted solution lasts only 10–12 hours (maker).
  - Signal and SLF-100: no job here (bloom potassium; salt removal).
  - TPS Seaweed Fertilizer: overlaps Root Boost's kelp.

## Open questions
