# putes log

## Left-out products (alternative; staged 2026-10-07; back if: a left-out product is reconsidered)

  - TPS One: a full fertilizer (see nitrogen).  
  - Silica Gold: evidence is mixed (silicon helped cucumbers against Pythium in Bélanger et al. 1991, but did nothing in a 2023 hydroponic lettuce study); it raises pH in unbuffered water, and TPS says to change silica solutions every 4–5 days.  
  - Dip 'N Grow: its maker says it's made for cuttings, and diluted solution must be used within 10–12 hours.  
  - Signal and SLF-100: no job here (bloom potassium; salt removal).  
  - TPS Seaweed Fertilizer (TPS Plant Foods, favored 2026-10-02) is Ascophyllum plus chlorella and spirulina with no guaranteed analysis, dosed 2–4 tsp/gal weekly; it overlaps Root Boost's kelp.  

## EC and composition detail (history; staged 2026-10-07; back if: EC or nutrient levels are re-derived or re-measured)

EC values are measured (½-gal batch, 2026-10-02). Day 7 skips RapidStart's measured +65 *(inference)*. Step sizes per mL/gal: Root Boost \~25, RapidStart \~32, HygroZyme \~3.  
**Measured batch, 2026-10-02 (½ gal, Measured):** base water + conditioner 0; CalMag 375; Root Boost 525; RapidStart 590; HygroZyme 620 µS/cm.

*(inference; Claude's label math)* Estimated composition: about 54 ppm N (about 44% ammonium), 24 P, 8 K, 33 Ca, 12 Mg, plus CalMag's micronutrients. Ca is 61% and Mg 22% of N, inside First Rays' ranges (Ca 30–75%, Mg 20–50%). On day 0, RapidStart adds about 5 N, 1 P and 4 K. N is below First Rays' 75–125 ppm weekly target for established plants.
