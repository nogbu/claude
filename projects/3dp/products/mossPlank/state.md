Details  
- A moss pole shaped like a plank (my description, 2026-10-09); moss-filled: the hollow is packed with damp sphagnum and roots grow in through the hexes (agreed 2026-10-10, my pick on a card over dry support or either way).  
- Base (my asks, 2026-10-10): for the 3.5in pot (nursery-pots: pot rev10.2, insert rev4); the plank's bottom sits level with the insert top (37.80 mm: 1.80 mm floor + 36.0 mm insert). Pot inner R at 37.80 mm: 39.34 mm (pot_profile). With nursery-pots agreed change 2 (inserts lean in 5.03°), the 3.5in insert's ceiling edge shrinks to R 25.98 mm (inference). 20% setback table moved to log.md.  

Requirements (from the research, 2026-10-10): met by the current design: stacks to extend, solid back and sides slow drying with an open front for air, prints without supports with a one-way D spigot, post on the insert so it doesn't lean. Watering drips stay over the pot (agreed 2026-10-10, my pick on a card): met, the plank's outline above the rim stays within the pot's inner wall at the lip base (max radius 44.88 mm vs 44.88, model, 3.5in pot). Proposed 2026-10-10, still to settle one at a time: moss stays in through 13.1 mm holes; packing and replacing moss; tie points for stems; width (3 to 5 in for heavy climbers, garden.org; plank 60 mm); translucent back to see moisture; adding to a planted pot.  
Research: quick pass and forum pass (2026-10-09/10) moved unchanged to log.md (Research, history); read it before turning findings into requirements.
