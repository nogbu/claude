## Product

* Replacement shelf for the UNICOLY glass cabinet (unicoly): a ClosetMaid MaxLoad 12" × 72" black wire shelf (Lowe's 10000-06675) cut into three roughly 24" sections, each with four printed corner pieces filling out the factory shelf's footprint. Barrina T5 clips: a later stage.  
* **Front**: the single-edge-rod side (crosswires end on it; J-slot piece). **Rear**: the lip side (two-rod piece).  
* By thickness, never by photo orientation: **crosswires** are the thin wires (2.9 mm); **support rods** the thick lengthwise rods (5.9 mm): front edge rod, two support rods, rear corner rod, lip outer rod. Rod positions and section depth are center to center unless stated.

## Requirements

* Match the factory shelf: 690 W × 300 D × 20 mm thick (Measured).  
* Design load: 40 lb per shelf, safety factor 3 (rail-project target, adopted 2026-09-30); per corner: agreed change 11.  
* Minimize the risk of human error.  
* All-printed, no bought hardware (rail's requirement for rail and clip designs; confirm it covers this shelf).  
* Warm, humid grow cabinet under sustained load raises PETG creep risk (inference).
