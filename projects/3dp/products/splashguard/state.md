splashguard  
Short name: splashguard

Current version  
- None yet (concept stage, started 2026-10-02).

Purpose (your brief, 2026-10-02)  
- A cover that goes around the plant stem at the top of a nursery pot. It keeps the pon in when the pot is tipped upside down, but lets water run out, ideally directed away from the fingers of the hand holding the pot. Imagined as something like a grommet.  
- Optimized for printing in TPU (94A on hand), assuming it needs to flex.  
- First version fits the 3.5in nursery pot (rev10.2); every pot size will get its own version.

Values and details  
- 3.5in rim (rev10.2, from nursery-pots, model values): lip 96.14 mm across and 4.32 mm tall; flat top; 2.032 mm vertical outer face; underside chamfer tucking 2.187 mm back to the wall over 2.288 mm of height (about 46°); wall 1.00 mm at 5.03°.  
- Opening inside the rim: about 89.8 mm (Estimated: 96.14 − 2 × (2.187 + 1.00)).  
- The pots hold anthuriums in pon and sit inside a decorative outer pot (nursery-pots).

Proposed concept (Claude, 2026-10-02; not agreed)  
- A disc that grips the pot rim, so it holds upside down.  
- Split from the edge to the center, so it fits around an existing plant without unpotting it.  
- Center grommet: radial flaps that flex around the stem, covering a range of stem sizes.  
- Drain slots on one side only, narrower than the smallest pon grain, ending in a small spout; the pot is held on the opposite side.
