# nursery-pots

Short name: nursery-pots. Self-watering pot family (2.5in, 3.5in, 4.5in, 6in), clear PETG, single-bead wall, for anthuriums in pon inside a decorative outer pot. Every size is scaled from the 3.5in pot (rev10); the 2.5in has its own foot, lip chamfer and floor. All sizes will go into one 3mf. 3.5in change history with reasons: 3.5pot-modlog.

Build scripts, all in projects/3dp/products/nursery-pots/build/. Shared: nurseryPots_lib.py and nurseryPots_settings_base.json (the pot files' project settings). One per file: 3.5inPot_rev10.2.py; 4.5inPot_rev3.py; 6inPot_rev2.py; 3.5inInsert_rev4.py; 4.5inInsert_rev1.py; 6inInsert_rev1.py; 6inInsert_rev2.py; 2.5inPot_rev2.py (same structure, rev2 values); 2.5in test scripts rev2.1–rev2.13 (list and needs below); 2.5inInsert_rev1.py (built 2026-10-09); 2.5inInsert_rev2.py (built 2026-10-10); 6inInsert_rev3.py (built 2026-10-10); 6inInsert_rev4.py (built 2026-10-10, 4 on a plate); 3.5inInsert_rev5.py (built 2026-10-10; needs 2.5inPot_rev2.py); insertMeasure.py and insertPicture.py (measure or draw any insert from its 3mf; need projects/3dp/scripts). 6inInsert_rev2.1_FanOff.py and 6inInsert_rev2.2_PotSettings.py (rev2's ceiling only; need trimesh and manifold3d); 6inInsert_rev2.3_DiscSettings.py (with its .stl and settings base); 6inInsert_rev2.4_DiscHoles.py and 6inInsert_rev2.5_MonoTop.py (need rev2.3's). 6inInsert_rev2.6_PotMonoTop.py (needs rev2.2's). To rebuild: put the two shared files, the script and any scripts it needs in one folder and run it (needs numpy, scipy, matplotlib). ## Shared design (rev10 family rules)

- **Wall angle**: 5.03° on every size (rev10's). Where a legacy height and top Ø are matched, the base Ø changes, not the angle. Matching them by scaling would change it (4.5in: 5.17°; 6.5in at 165 mm: 5.73°); 5.03° keeps the family's look and slightly enlarges the reservoir (2026-09-30).
- **Foot (not scaled)**: 45° chamfer to 5.12 mm, then a 10 mm radius curve into the wall, ending at 11.31 mm. Fan ramp 9.72 / 10.44 / 11.16 / 11.88 mm. Chamfer and curve rise a fixed 6.19 mm together, so both ends can't sit on the 0.36 mm grid without changing the radius (inference, 2026-09-30); left as is. Unscaled so it prints the same and the fan ramp heights carry over (2026-09-29).
- **Lip (not scaled)**: 4.408 mm tall (4.32 mm, 12 layers, from rev10.2, rev3 and rev2), overhanging the wall 2.187 mm; it sets the top Ø. Vertical outer face 2.12 → 2.032 mm, chamfer unchanged (decided 2026-09-30).
- **Splash guard**: splashguard grips each size's lip, so a lip change needs a matching guard change (2026-10-02).
- **Wall**: smooth (no seam groove), single bead: set the object's outer and inner wall line width to at least the wall thickness so Arachne keeps one bead (set per object from the 2026-09-30 build: 1.05 mm on 3.5in and 4.5in, 1.2 mm on 6in). The slot band prints 2 beads via a height range modifier (0.45 mm on 3.5in and 4.5in, 0.6 mm on 6in in the current files; 0.54 and 0.64 mm from rev10.2, rev3 and rev2). 1.2 mm is the thickest safe single bead on the 0.6 mm nozzle (2× nozzle).
- **Drip groove**: ring channel under the floor. 3.5in: 1.5 mm wide × 0.72 mm deep at r 23.5–25 mm; larger sizes: 1.5 mm wide, outer edge 5 mm inside the base edge. Floor ridge above it: 0.5 mm wider each side, 0.72 mm tall, top at 2.16 mm (inferred from the reservoir volumes); with the 1.80 mm floor (3.5in rev10.2, 4.5in rev3, 6in rev2) it stands 0.36 mm above the floor. Agreed change for the next 3.5in, 4.5in and 6in builds (2026-10-03, not built): groove 2.0 mm wide × 1.08 mm deep (3 layers), widened outward (inner edge stays 6.5 mm, outer edge 4.5 mm inside the base edge; 3.5in: Ø 47–51 mm); ridge 3.0 mm wide (3.5in: Ø 46–52 mm), top 2.16 → 2.52 mm (7 layers). Floor stays 1.80 mm, so 1.44 mm of plastic covers the groove. Floor height range 0–2.52 mm; reservoir range starts at 2.52 mm. 3.5in reservoir about −0.2 mL (inference). The insert sits on the floor outside the ridge, so its height doesn't change. Why: the 0.72 mm groove can barely be felt and may be too small to stop a drip (inference; untested); sizes chosen by me. nurseryPots_lib.py hard-codes the groove depth (0.72), ridge top (2.16) and the floor range's 2.16 mm: the next build must change them or make them parameters.
- **Reservoir waterproofing** (floor to slots): seam gap 0%, outer wall flow ratio 1.03, outer wall 28 mm/s. The band layer just below the slots also gets seam gap 0% and flow 1.03 (stays 40 mm/s). A normal seam with these passed the water test in the foot (rev9.8.2); the scarf did not.
- **Slot band**: 40 mm/s, 0.36 layers, outer-then-inner walls, wipe before the outer loop. Height ranges run from 0.558 mm below the slot bottom to 0.542 mm above the slot top, split at 0.008 mm below the bottom and 0.242 mm above the top (2026-09-29).
- **Insert** (every size): no gussets; a cylinder with a 1.24 mm gap each side at the rim, sitting on the pot floor (matches insert_rev3). Wall matches the pot's slot band (thickness and 2-bead width). Ceiling: Ø 4 mm center hole; 4 radial slots with fully rounded ends at 0/90/180/270°, in line with the side notches; flush Ø 4 mm diagonal holes (no tubes). 4 side notches open at the rim, fully rounded ends (end radius = half the width). 4.5in and 6in scale with diameter (×1.387 and ×1.786; see Decisions); notch heights round to the nearest 0.4 mm layer (decided 2026-09-30). The 6in has 8 diagonal holes (log: Insert design, sizes and insert_rev3 geometry). Height: Product rules, in whole 0.4 mm layers.

## Decisions and why

- **Normal seam instead of scarf (rev10)**: supersedes the earlier V-groove seam design. The scarf leaked in the foot flare; its settings, placement and tests are in the log (Scarf seam settings).
- **6in insert slope 5.03° and small shoulder** (2026-10-08): chosen by me over 7.70° (legacy inserts) and 10°, and over medium and large shoulders (log: 6in insert rev2 options).
- **Inserts scaled** (2026-09-30): the 4.5in and 6in inserts scale the radial slots, chamfer, curve, notch width and height, and hole positions with diameter; holes stay Ø 4 mm; the 6in gets a second hole on each diagonal. Chosen over fixed-size features for proportion; wider slots accepted.
