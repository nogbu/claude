# **product.6in-pot**

Short name: 6in-pot. The 6 inch self-watering pot, scaled from the 3.5in pot (rev10). Same material, use and seam method as the 3.5in pot (see product.3.5in-pot).

## **Latest rev**

**rev1** (newPot_6in_rev1.3mf), built 2026-09-29. Not yet printed.

## **Key parameters and dimensions (rev1)**

> * **Size**: uniform scale 1.5852 of rev10: 166.63 mm tall, top Ø 152.4 mm, base Ø 105.43 mm; wall angle 5.03° (same as rev10).  
> * **Foot**: same as rev10 (45° chamfer to 5.12 mm, 10 mm radius curve ending at 11.31 mm); fan ramp stays at 9.72–11.88 mm.  
> * **Wall**: 1.2 mm single bead, with the object's outer and inner wall line width set to 1.2 mm. Floor 1.44 mm. Lip the same size as rev10 (4.408 mm tall, 2.187 mm overhang).  
> * **Slots**: 8 slots, 2.5 × 12 mm, 43.0–55.0 mm.  
> * **Reservoir**: 470 mL empty.  
> * **Height ranges**: floor 0–2.2 mm; reservoir waterproofing 2.2–42.442 mm; slot band 42.442–55.542 mm (two 0.6 mm beads).  
> * **Plastic**: 98.5 cm³ (125.1 g).

## **Decisions and why**

> * Waterline 43.0 mm: chosen from options at 43.76 mm (the same % step as 3.5in → 4.5in) and 44.0 mm (legacy).  
> * Slots 2.5 × 12 mm: chosen by me, instead of scaled (2.4 × 12.7 mm).  
> * Wall 1.2 mm: the thickest safe single bead on the 0.6 mm nozzle (2× nozzle).

## **Legacy reference**

> * No legacy 6in pot. The nearest is the legacy 6.5in (see product.6.5in-pot).

## **Open questions**

> 1. Water test: the waterproofing settings were proven on a 1.05 mm wall, not 1.2 mm.  
> 2. Outer wall speed: the 12 mm³/s cap limits a 1.2 mm bead at 0.36 mm layers to about 28 mm/s, so the 34 mm/s outer wall setting can't be reached (inference).  
> 3. Insert for this size (parked).
