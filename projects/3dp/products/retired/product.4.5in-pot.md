# **product.4.5in-pot**

Short name: 4.5in-pot. The 4.5 inch self-watering pot, scaled from the 3.5in pot (rev10) to the legacy 4.5in pot's top diameter and height. Same material, use and seam method as the 3.5in pot (see product.3.5in-pot).

## **Latest rev**

**rev2** (newPot_4.5in_rev2.3mf), built 2026-09-30. Not yet printed. rev1 (newPot_4.5in_rev1.3mf) was the same pot with a 5.17° wall angle.

## **Key parameters and dimensions (rev2)**

> * **Size**: 129.98 mm tall, top Ø 122.25 mm, base Ø 81.73 mm; wall angle 5.03° (same as rev10).  
> * **Foot**: rev10 foot (45° chamfer to 5.12 mm, 10 mm radius curve ending at 11.31 mm); fan ramp stays at 9.72–11.88 mm.  
> * **Wall**: 1.00 mm, single bead. Floor 1.44 mm. Lip the same size as rev10 (not scaled).  
> * **Slots**: 8 slots, 2.0 × 10 mm, 38.0–48.0 mm.  
> * **Reservoir**: 265.7 mL empty.  
> * **Drip groove**: inner edge 6.5 mm and outer edge 5 mm inside the base edge; the ridge on the floor above it is 0.5 mm wider on each side.  
> * **Height ranges**: floor 0–2.2 mm; reservoir waterproofing 2.2–37.442 mm; slot band 37.442–48.542 mm (two 0.45 mm beads).  
> * **Plastic**: 53.0 cm³ (67.3 g).

## **Decisions and why**

> * rev2: wall angle changed from 5.17° to 5.03° to match rev10 (+2.9 mL).  
> * Wall 1.00 mm: same as rev10.1's T5.  
> * Waterline 38.0 mm.
