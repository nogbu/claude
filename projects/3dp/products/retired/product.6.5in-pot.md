## **Decisions and why**

> * Height 165 mm, 10 mm shorter than the legacy pot: with the angle fixed at 5.03°, the shorter pot has a wider base and holds 14 mL more than a 175 mm version (543 mL).  
> * Wall angle 5.03° to match rev10 (a 5.73° angle was considered).  
> * Waterline 38.0 mm (43.0 mm would give 638 mL).  
> * Slots 2.5 × 12 mm, same as the 6in pot.  
> * Wall 1.2 mm, same as the 6in pot.
