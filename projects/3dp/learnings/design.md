# learnings/design

Moved from learnings.building (2026-10-07).

> * Concept pictures (2026-10-01): Shaded 3D views, cutaways and sections come from Claude's numpy renderer, projects/3dp/scripts/render3d.py (vectorized 2026-10-07: 3 to 5 times faster per view than calibration-stand's renderlib.py, six stand pictures match the old ones to within 33 pixels; renderlib.py is a shim onto it). Near-top-down views lit from the camera side read best. Use it, not matplotlib's 3D plots, which drew the rig's faces in the wrong order (2026-10-02).  
> * Section concepts (2026-10-02): for a part best seen in profile (a pot inside a cache pot), a 2D matplotlib section drawn from the real profiles reads clearly (projects/3dp/products/cache-pot/build/cachePot_concept.py). Such 2D views from the shapely profiles take seconds and far less usage than shaded 3D renders (2026-10-05). Put labels in empty space and check for overlaps; the first cache pot drawing took three redraws.  
> * Test-piece labels (2026-10-01): wire-slot-test-piece_rev1's rib/pip labels (PETG-CF, 0.6 nozzle) were too small to read; slots were counted from the narrow end.  
> * Explanatory pictures of a built part (2026-10-01): the counting picture for wire-slot-test-piece_rev1 was drawn from memory, showed 4 of 11 slots, and called the doc's ribs and pips "ridges" and "bumps".  
> * Printed spring stiffness (2026-10-01, rfid rev2): a single-bead PETG serpentine measured about 0.66× the beam model's stiffness (129 g measured vs. about 196 g modeled with Bambu PETG Translucent's data-sheet stiffness; JAYO filament, inference). Scale force estimates by a measured factor, per filament and fold size.  
> * Lay out before quoting (2026-10-01): an rfid force estimate assumed a side guide before the layout was drawn, and the picture showed it wouldn't fit.  
> * Derived sizes (2026-10-01): an rfid rev2 fold table assumed rev1's 3.5 mm end blocks; at 25 mm wide the 26 mm end arcs make them 4.7 mm, which cut the fold gap.  
> * Flush first (2026-10-05, calibration-stand): under dp 3, Claude kept steps with reasons it made up, and recommended a step to save filament.  
> * Picture every option (2026-10-05, calibration-stand): the feet card implied that lowering the feet removed the step; the drawing showed the step only moved to x 115.  
> * Section stress check (2026-10-05, calibration-stand): a 450 MPa bending figure for 1.86 mm cradle walls came from two errors: r12 outside corners over r4 pocket corners, which cut through the wall, and an extreme-fiber distance fixed at 18 mm. Take that distance from each section's own neutral axis. Treat a figure far past the material's strength as a model error to find before quoting.  
> * Equal walls for elbow pairs (2026-10-05, calibration-stand): the r17 / r1 elbow inference used the 16 mm post and missed the 18 mm bar, and the lap the outer curve would cut into.
> * Joint comparisons (2026-10-05, calibration-stand): list only the advantages that matter for the job. When every option clears the design load by a wide margin, strength doesn't decide it; locating the parts at glue-up (dp 10) and simplicity do. Claude argued a half-lap + V key on strength, leaving dp 10 out until the user raised it.
> * Side-printed keys (2026-10-05, calibration-stand): a key or tenon that sticks out sideways from a part printed on its side starts as a strand over air at its lowest edge, held only at its root. The diamond tenon proposed for the side frames would have started as an 8 mm strand. Put the key where it sits on the bed, with 45 deg sides, and the slot in the other part, its roof at 45 deg.
> * Check the edge exists (2026-10-05, calibration-stand): change 32 sized an r1 inside corner where the foot meets the post, but the low clip block fills that corner, so the edge doesn't exist.  
> * Edges near markers (2026-10-05, calibration-stand): changes 33 and 37 sized the rail chamfers and post corners from a third of a feature and never checked photo-board, which requires a one-cell (1.86 mm) white margin around every marker. 2.1 and 1.2 chamfers and r4 corners cut into or nearly touched that margin. Read the product that sets a surface's job before sizing its edges, once brought in (config.3dp, Product scope).
> * Measuring text strokes (2026-10-05, plantTags): measuring strokes by how much area survives an inward offset understated them (0.5–0.9 mm reported, 0.7–1.0 mm actual), because the offset thins thick strokes too. Measure strokes with an opening (offset in, then out by the same amount) and gaps with a closing, and check against the font's stem widths. Bambu Studio text parts store their font and size (the em size) in model_settings.config; the font can come from google/fonts on raw.githubusercontent.com and be measured with fontTools (Overpass Heavy = wght 800).  
> * Printed weight (2026-10-05, calibration-stand): size tipping and weight checks from printed weight (walls, top/bottom layers and infill at the process settings), not solid volume. Solid parts overstated the stand's weight 2.3 times (1,805 g vs 785 g), and its tipping force with it.
> * Fold-in redraw (2026-10-05, calibration-stand): drawing every agreed change together in clean8.py found four edges no change had sized: the clip block corners, the rail-to-end edge, the side-to-end corners and a 0.64 step under the low clip.
> * Brace filling a corner (2026-10-06, calibration-stand): an 18 brace whose lower face is fixed by the clip ceiling meets the post 2.6 above the ledge, so the corner above it can't stay a useful hole; any hole left a sub-bead web under the top tenon.
> * Turning a part over for a picture (2026-10-08, nursery-pots): map print (x, y, z) to (x, −y, H − z), a rotation; (x, y, H − z) mirrors the part. Symmetric parts hide it, but text reads backward: the 6in insert's size mark was redrawn three times. Check text or any handed feature before the first render.
> * Close-up crops (2026-10-08, shelf): filtering render faces by position drops faces that span the cut and leaves the close-up hollow; crop the model with a manifold3d intersection and render that.
> * Model what crosses a joint first (2026-10-09, shelf): the keyhole neck was sized as if each crosswire passed through it end-on; photo 20260930_141955 shows the wires lie across the rod top and run back, so they would cut through both neck lips. Model the mating parts, from photos, before sizing a joint around them.
> * Hold-down cantilevers (2026-10-09, shelf): a 1.2 mm roof reaching the full sleeve depth came out about 250 MPa (inference); covering only 2.0 mm of the wire cut its arm and needed 2.5–3.1 mm. Size a cantilever's length to the contact it needs before its thickness.
> * Wall carrying a cantilever (2026-10-09, shelf): a cut through the wall takes the moment about the wall's own mid-thickness. The front wall's 2.25 mm need used the arm to its inner face (2.6 instead of 3.8 mm); it held only because the 3D spread over 13–16 mm.
> * Short, deep hold-downs (2026-10-09, shelf): a roof reaching 2.6 mm on a 3.1 mm thickness peaked 1.7–2.5× the beam formula at its root fillet (3D FE). Check such roots with a 2D model calibrated to one 3D run before sizing. A flexible sleeve sends load to the nearest contacts: a third crosswire picked up almost nothing. Drop an option whose 2D margin is under that gap; it won't pass in 3D.
> * Spots inside an assembly (2026-10-09, calibration-stand): the seam tenons sit inside the assembled frames, so coloring their faces showed nothing until the frames were pulled 45 out sideways. Explode or hide covering parts before coloring a spot inside an assembly.
> * Check the script before calling a feature missing (2026-10-09, calibration-stand): audit change 165 asked to add the rings' r0.9 floor-to-wall curve, which fitpieces.py ring() had cut since 2026-10-06; the audit read only the doc's ring line. Open the script that makes the part before calling something absent.
> * Roof root fixes split by direction (2026-10-09, shelf): a thicker front wall cut only the along-the-layers peak (wall bending); the across peak at the roof tip fell only with a thicker roof. Check both load placements (wire bearing over the overlap and at the roof tip): the lead passed one and failed the other. The 2D corner model read 2–5 MPa under the 3D.
> * Coloring a spot after render3d.load (2026-10-09, shelf): load drops degenerate triangles, so recolor FACES by a test on each face's centroid and normal, never by the mesh's face index. A blind bore's inside shows only in a model cut through it (trimesh slice_plane with cap) seen from the cut side.
> * Seated fit before a test piece (2026-10-10, mossPlank): joint test piece rev1 went to print with the stub's straight spigot overlapping the post's tapered socket by 1,123 mm³; the 0.2 mm stub stops 2.3 mm in of 30. Before building mating parts, intersect them seated (overlap 0) and along the insertion path.
