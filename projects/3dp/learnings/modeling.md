# learnings/modeling

Started 2026-10-10 (shelf) while learnings/design was at its cap.

> * Sizes from the doc (2026-10-10, shelf): rear-piece_model.py measured close_below from the lip bore center, but agreed change 28 says 1.75 under the lip rod's center, and the rod rests 0.15 lower, so the wire cleared 0.15 instead of 0.30 through agreed change 31's 3D runs and the first sleeve option pictures. Before using a model size, check it's measured from the point the doc names (rod center, bore center, wire end), and name that point in the parameter's docstring.
> * Cuts meeting a faceted bore (2026-10-10, shelf): a cut ending on the true circle, or on a 45° line meant to touch it, left 0.003–0.016 mm steps on the 64-segment bore that dpcheck flagged as off the layer grid; it took four tries. Take every point where a cut meets a bore from the facets (rear-piece_model.py bore_z); learnings/fe has the 3D side.
> * Lead-in at a peaked bore's mouth (2026-10-10, shelf): keep the cone inside the roof's 45° lines carried down (rear-piece_model.py lead_c). Clipped only at the flat, it widened the 1.67 bridge to 1.99 at the mouth; a box clip at the 45° start left an off-grid step. The front piece's build meets the same case (agreed changes 15, 20).
> * Bore opening into a side slot (2026-10-10, shelf): printed on its side, the strip of wall between a 45°-peaked bore and the slot it opens into starts on the print-top side as a hairline over air, held only at the slot's closed end. The slicer skips its layers while they're thinner than a bead, then lays full-length lines over air: front-fit-test_rev1's layer 34 went down as two 31 mm lines along the sleeve (one already slowed to 11 mm/s) and printed as strings (G-code, photos). OrcaSlicer had flagged it as a floating cantilever, and it was kept as a dp exception. Cutting the strip and the roof flat away so the bore's far 45° face runs on to the outer face (front-piece_model.py open_corner) sliced with no warning and no added depth, but the bore then no longer holds the rod toward that side.
> * Corners from the profile's own faces (2026-10-10, calibration-stand): 134's tip fill started from a typed corner point 0.046 outside the snap U's tab face (the U is a section of the barrinaClip mesh), which left a 0.046 step 3 mm along the tab and a zero-thickness sliver. Find a corner from the profile's face lines (wm/clipfeat.py tip_corner), and make a fillet cutter reach past both faces into air.
> * Edge profiles judged per segment (2026-10-10, calibration-stand): fitlib.py boundary_runs judges a whole straight segment at its midpoint, so shortening the hinge slot (129) moved the midpoint of its block side into the U and flipped the whole face from the 0.9 profile to sharp. Decide edges by explicit feature rules, slot faces first (fitpieces.py sharp_edges), and check the runs in a picture after any feature moves.
> * Mating faces in a sliced model (2026-10-10, calibration-stand): the working model's board, built from 0.1 slabs, stair-stepped its tenons' 45 deg faces into the frame slots (10.75 mm3 overlap). Build mating faces as exact prisms and union them after the slabs (wm140.py board()).
> * Edge-profile bands that end mid-boundary (2026-10-10, calibration-stand): flat-capped bands left 34 four-face edges where the board's profile changed near the rail corners; round caps (wm140.py band_round) cleared them. The fit test clips' flat caps leave pinch vertices too, harmless only while the mesh keeps manifold3d's vertices (learnings/building).
> * Model check on a stepped scratch model (2026-10-11, calibration-stand): dpcheck.py flagged about 1,000 items per fit test clip, nearly all from fitlib.py's 0.1 bevel slabs, and read r1.0 curves turning 45 deg as r0.3 to r0.7. To find what a change added, run the check on the version before it too (oldpieces.py) and compare by feature; check a flagged curve with a circle fit on the profile.
> * Axis swaps in a warp (2026-10-10, mossPlank): a manifold3d warp that only swaps two axes, (x, y, z) to (x, z, y), mirrors the solid and turns it inside out; the plate's hex cut then added material (32.7 cm³ instead of 6.3) and dpcheck read 37 false overhangs. Swap plus mirror one axis, e.g. (x, d + depth − z, y), as hex_holes does, and check the volume after any warp. For a face-down print of a face at the part's lowest y, trimesh rotation_matrix(+π/2, [1, 0, 0]) maps y to z; −π/2 puts that face on top.
