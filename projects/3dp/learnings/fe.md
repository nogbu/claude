# learnings/fe

3D check learnings for the 3dp project; started 2026-10-10 from two build learnings (moved) when learnings/design reached its cap.

> * Model for a 3D check (2026-10-09, shelf, build/fe/rear_tet.py): boxes in the manifold3d model meet exactly, and cuts follow the faceted bore, not the true circle. 0.01 mm overlaps left sliver edges; tetgen refined around them to twice the tets and the solver stopped converging after 20 minutes. A trim cut to the true circle's height left a 0.02 mm step on the facets that read 136 MPa (a figure from the faulty element below; the sliver lesson stands). Check the model's shortest edge (under 0.05 mm is a sliver) before meshing.
> * Rod contact (2026-10-09, shelf): a pressure patch on a faceted bore loads the crease between facets, and its peak grew with every refinement (85 to 186 MPa, from the faulty element below), tapered ends or not. Check a rod bearing in a hole by Hertz (rear-sleeve_beam.py hertz()); read 3D only away from the contact.
> * Memory and meshing (2026-10-09, shelf): assembling in 20,000-element chunks with unsmoothed aggregation (pyamg smooth=None) ran 1.24 million unknowns at 6.0 GB peak; smoothed aggregation ran out of memory at 14 GB on 668,424, and whole-mesh assembly would need about 13 GB at 270,000 tets (inference). tetgen 0.8.4's background sizing mesh (bgmesh) segfaults; refine the surface near the spot instead (rear_tet.py refine_near).
> * Element check (2026-10-10, shelf, build/fe/rear_tet.py, front_twist_tet.py): rear_tet.py built the shape gradients with inv(J) transposed, and front_twist_tet.py copied it, so every stress and deflection from them was wrong, agreed change 31's included. An audit caught it with a quadratic field on one tet, whose gradient must come out exact. Both scripts now assert that at start (dNdx); give any new element code the same check before trusting a run.
> * Meshing a manifold3d model (2026-10-10, shelf, front-twist_model.py): boxes unioned face to face left float32 twin vertices (zero-length edges), and tetgen 0.8.4 then failed without an error. Build one envelope minus voids, run each void's outline past the face it opens, keep cuts from sharing lines on the bore's facets, and weld vertices (trimesh merge_vertices, 4 digits) and check watertight before meshing (front_twist_tet.py).
> * Sharp inside edges (2026-10-10, shelf): an inside edge without a fillet is a singularity whose peak grows with refinement; read it at fixed distances (0.5 and 1 mm) and compare runs at the same distance, or fillet it in the model.
> * Open sections (2026-10-10, shelf, twist-lock front piece): a hook curled over a rod and open at the rear is a C section, weak in twist. Beam estimates gave its top 15 to 26 MPa; 3D gave 67 to 123 at the curl root and a 3.5 mm lift. Check twist in 3D before sizing any open section loaded off its shear center.
> * Readouts near load bands (2026-10-10, shelf, front_twist_post.py profile()): a peak read 2 mm clear of the load bands can sit on that limit while the stress keeps rising into the band (an independent check: a quoted 98 was 112 to 180 nearer the load). Profile the stress into the band before quoting a peak, and read shear between layers and across the layers along with tension.
> * Refining (2026-10-10, shelf, twist-lock D): the refined mesh (--fine 0.5) raised the hook's lift 25% over the coarse one; quote refined values for a pass or fail. When tetgen stops with "Internal TetGen error within split_subface" (a 0.007 mm surface edge), change the surface edge length (front_twist_tet.py --rl 1.9 meshed where 2.0 failed).
