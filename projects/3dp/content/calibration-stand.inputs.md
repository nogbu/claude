Stand inputs (agreed 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-04 in the filament-calibration thread; used by the small stand; sizes are placeholders unless tagged)  
- Phone: bare Pixel 10 Pro, 152.8 x 72.0 x 8.6 mm, 207 g (Listed, Google UK spec page; GSMArena lists 8.5 mm). Camera bar 65 mm across x 22 mm along the phone, its top edge 13 mm from the phone's top edge (Measured). Main lens center about 26 mm from the top edge and 28 mm from the left edge, seen from the back (Measured). Bump height 3.3 mm (Notebookcheck) vs 4.0 mm (Gorilla Case blog): the sources disagree, so the bar recess is 4.5 mm deep. Main camera 82 deg diagonal, 24 mm equivalent, 4:3, 50 MP mode (Listed, Google). Corners about r11.5 to r12.5 in plan (Measured 2026-10-06 from a pen trace on dot paper, photo 20261006_011047; pen offset 0.45 to 0.75 removed by inference), so the pocket's r4 corners can't bind. Edge radius not measured; the fit test's pocket rings check it.  
- Black insert: matte black cardstock cut to the 135 channel, about 0.3 thick (Estimated, 2026-10-06; measure a sheet), well under the 1.2 of flat stop and rail face that 121 and 125 leave.  
- Lights: four Barrina T5 grow light bars (Amazon B0CHRN1FFK, 4-pack, owned): 1 ft, 5 W, 5000K, 120 deg beam, linkable, magnetic mounts and clips (Listed, barrina-led.com, NC05); about 298 x 20 x 20 mm, and a plugged-in cord bent 90 deg sticks out 33 mm past the end cap (Measured). CRI is not published. 1 ft bar weight not measured; 63 g assumed (half the 126 g measured for a 2 ft bar in barrina-clips, inference).  
  - Glare rule (inference, from the lighting research 2026-10-04): with the lens at H above the board, a light at height z and distance r from the lens axis stays out of a region of half-width a only if r > a(1 + z/H). The channel's half-width is 108.5 mm.  
- Load: design load 20 N down plus 10 N sideways, safety factor 3, so parts are sized for 60 N + 30 N (the user's choice). PLA Matte data sheet (Listed): bending strength 53 MPa along layers, 32 across; bending modulus 2,360 / 2,040 MPa. Bambu PLA Matte density 1.32 g/cm³ (Listed, Orca's "Bambu PLA Matte @base" profile, checked 2026-10-05).  
- Camera routine (review item 10): manual focus, ISO, white balance about 5000K and shutter set per light mode; 3 s timer or voice shutter. Focus on the board, except refocus on the max-speed comb's top. Each session, take one empty-board photo per light mode. Room's overhead light off (a ceiling lamp 2 m up within about 830 mm of the lens axis puts glare on glossy pieces, inference).  
- Build script checks (review item 11), run on every rev: nothing lit or Bone White inside the camera view; the glare rule with 3 mm margin on every lit face point; lens height from the camera bump; 0.5 mm board clearance; 45 deg overhang check per part as printed; plate fit including the excluded corner; grid lock; a step finder (every height change, dp 3), an edge inventory (every edge sized or a listed exception, dp 2), a dense photo-edge check (the camera view along every edge, not sample points) and a printability check (overhangs and bridges per part as printed) (change 102, 2026-10-05); then an Orca slice for real times and grams.  
- Bar details (2026-10-04): cross-section 20 x 20 mm (Measured). The LEDs are on one flat 20 mm face. The two faces with notches are the sides; the face nearer the notches is the top, and the face farthest from them is the bottom, the LED face (the user's description). Notch size from the user's clip 2234567.3mf: its snap bumps are triangles 2 mm deep and 4 mm tall, tip 4 mm below the top face, so each notch is at least that size (inference, assumes the clip fits the notch).  
- Switch (Measured 2026-10-04, photos PXL_20261004_070704287 to 070750083, scaled from the bar's 20 mm height, about +/-1 mm): a rocker on one notched side face, just below the notch; bezel about 40-55 mm from the end cap shown and about 5-15 mm down from the top face. The tube wall needs a slot from the open end to the switch. The bars have plugs at both ends (the user, 2026-10-04), so each bar can go in with its switch facing up and its cord leaving at the back; the switch then sits near the back on one side and near the front on the other.  
- Phone buttons (Measured 2026-10-04, the user): on the right edge, 40 to 80 mm from the top edge, so 72.8 mm is clear at the USB end; a 35 mm right stop clears them by about 38 mm (inference).
