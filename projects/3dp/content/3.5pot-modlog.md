# 3.5in pot: modification log (for the simplification pass)

Current reference: **rev10.1** (newPot_rev10.1.3mf) = rev10 with T5 (wall 1.00 mm) and T6 (waterline 33.0 mm); printed 2026-09-30, water test passed. Current state, open questions and rev10.2's agreed changes are in nursery-pots.

Every design, settings and filament change to the 3.5in pot while testing, with why. At simplification, each is re-tested to decide whether it stays.

Status key: **kept** = in rev10.1 · **test** = queued for testing · **closed** = tried and dropped.
