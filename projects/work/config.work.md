config.work  
Short name: work

Cardinal sources  
- Official Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising documentation and help-center articles, for how match types, negative keywords, bidding, targeting and reporting actually work.  
- The client's own official websites and product pages, for product names, plans, eligibility and brand terms.  
- The account's own data (exports, performance reports, search terms reports), weighted over industry benchmarks or third-party opinion.

Rules

Client docs  
- Each work client gets a client doc: projects/work/products/[name]/state.md. It's this project's product doc (the product skill applies to it), built around the client's latest account export.  
- Beyond what the product skill lists, it holds: the client and its accounts (platform, account ID, how it's managed); the latest export (source, date, what it covers); product context (what they sell, eligibility, brand and product names), with sources; naming taxonomy (campaign and ad group name patterns, exceptions and inconsistencies); account structure (geo groups, campaign categories, active campaigns with type, bidding, budget and launch date, ad group and keyword structure, audiences, shared lists and where they're attached); goals and which conversions count; and links to its performance snapshots and audits.  
- Rules for one client (its goal, brand terms to protect, campaigns to ignore) go in its client doc's Product rules section, never in config.work.  
- When I share a new export, compare it with the client doc and list what changed before updating. Figures taken from an export are tagged Measured with the export date.  
- Performance snapshots and audits stay in their own content files, linked from the client doc.  
- Paused campaigns are for reference only: leave them out of every analysis unless I ask to include them.
