## AARP plan and enrollment details (parked; staged 2026-10-07; back if: a review needs plan prices, eligibility, membership terms or site wording)
- Eligibility: AARP members only, with guaranteed acceptance and no health questions. "Active AARP members and their spouse or domestic partner, partner in a civil union, and unmarried dependent children and grandchildren under age 26" can enroll. Enrollment is open any time: online, by phone at (866) 583-2085 (Mon–Fri, 8 am–9 pm ET) or by mail.
- AARP membership: open to anyone 18 or older. It's $15 for the first year with automatic renewal (Listed, aarp.org, 2026-10-06).
