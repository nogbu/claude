# Delta Dental Campaign Negatives: item-by-item review (complete)
Companion to negative-keyword-audit.md. This doc supersedes the 42-entry list there: the list is now **57 keywords** (42 originals kept + 15 plan names added).

This list's review finished on 2026-09-28.

## AARP plan names
The AARP Dental Insurance Plan comes in four options, all administered by Delta Dental:
- PPO Protect Plus
- PPO Protect Propel
- PPO Protect
- DeltaCare USA Essential (DHMO; 19 states + DC only)

Older plan names still seen by some enrollees: PPO Plan A and PPO Plan B.

These run on the Delta Dental PPO and DeltaCare USA networks.

Sources:
- https://www1.deltadentalins.com/aarp/dental-plans.html (Plans at a glance, and the cost-per-service table)
- https://www1.deltadentalins.com/aarp.html ("With a choice of PPO plans and a DeltaCare® USA plan…")
- https://www1.deltadentalins.com/aarp/faqs.html ("The PPO plan options — PPO Protect Plus, PPO Protect Propel and PPO Protect…"; also PPO Plan A and PPO Plan B)
