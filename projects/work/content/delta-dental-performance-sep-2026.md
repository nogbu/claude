## Findings
1. Brand carried the month: 75% of enrollments (469 of 625) at $179, down from $240 in August, while spend rose 47%. It still lost 34% of search impression share to budget, so it likely has room to scale (inference).
2. PMax CPA more than doubled ($102 to $226) and is above its Target CPA ($145 NENT, $165 ENT) on 75% more spend, with fewer enrollments (168 to 132).
3. NB Enroll's first full month: $14,090 for 10 enrollments ($1,409). The two NB Remarketing campaigns and NB NENT ASI spent $8,658 for 2.
4. CO got worse: $575 CPA vs $406, on flat spend.
5. DG was cut from $9,662 to $1,295 and still converted nothing (18,960 clicks).
6. Overall CPA held flat ($220 vs $217) because Brand's gains offset PMax's and NB's losses.
These reinforce perf-30d recommendations 2–5 (inference).
