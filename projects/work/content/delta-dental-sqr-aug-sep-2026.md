August NB was mostly the legacy 45+ campaigns paused around Aug 30 (inference), so NB's Aug → Sep change is a campaign-set swap, not a trend.
- CO group: competitor-name terms $2,881 (40,405 impressions, +14% vs August); generic insurance $1,228; Delta terms $1,071. "zocdoc dentist" is 50% of CO impressions and 62% of cost; Physicians Mutual, Ameritas, Mutual of Omaha and Spirit searches (4,028 → 1,738 impressions) arrive only as broad matches of it, since CO's keywords cover Aetna, Aspen, Cigna, Humana, MetLife, UnitedHealthcare and Zocdoc.

## Callouts (2026-10-09)
1. Search grew on price as much as volume: +37% impressions, +39% clicks, +66% cost, CPC $2.11 → $2.51. perf-sep's search campaigns went from about 262 to 493 enrollments (+88%) and search CPA from about $256 to $216 (inference: perf-sep totals minus PMax and DG), so better click-to-enrollment absorbed the CPC rise.
2. Brand took the money (+71%, Enroll plus RAQ; Brand Enroll alone $84,116 per perf-sep), rising every week; "delta dental ppo" and the AARP terms drove it.
3. NB: two keywords take 38% of its cost ("dental insurance no waiting period", "dental care" at a 6% CTR); brand leak 11%. perf-sep: 10 enrollments at $1,409.
4. CO is flat and "zocdoc dentist" dominates it; the account's biggest term declines are its broad matches. The report can't explain CO's CPA rise ($406 → $575).
5. "dental plans for over 65" in Brand draws NB-type searches ($4,085).
6. October: $6,936 a day vs $5,806 (+19%), mostly more clicks (+15% a day), CPC $2.60 vs $2.51 (seven days).
- Caveats: hidden "Other search terms" were about 18% of Search spend in the deleted Aug 1 – Oct 7 manager pull ($70,466 of $392,397; inference); "All conv." ignored.
