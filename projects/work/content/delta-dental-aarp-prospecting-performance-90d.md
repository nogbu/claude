# Delta Dental – AARP SEM: Prospecting performance, Jun 29 – Sep 27, 2026
Source: Google Ads ad group report (weekly), 13 weeks. Metrics: Enrollments (New) is the primary KPI; RAQ (New) is quote requests.

## Totals
- Prospecting campaigns: $199.6k spend, 882 enrollments, $226 CPA; 19.9k quote requests.
- Last 4 weeks (Aug 31 – Sep 27): $105.2k spend, 513 enrollments, $205 CPA.
- The report's manager-account total for the same period is $1.05M spend and 5,032 enrollments, so Prospecting is about 19% of account spend.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Key findings
- Brand CPA held at about $163–175 while weekly spend grew from $9k to $26k. Brand impression share is only about 32%, so there may be room to scale; the split of lost impression share between budget and rank is still needed to confirm.
- The new non-brand campaigns convert quote requests at half the legacy rate (11.6% vs 25.0%). Their CPC is also half ($1.36 vs $2.94), consistent with Maximize clicks buying cheaper, lower-intent traffic.
- NB_General is weak: ENT $1,141 CPA, NENT $451.
- Enroll vs RAQ duplicate campaigns show no consistent winner: ENT RAQ $151 vs ENT Enroll $191; NENT Enroll $174 vs NENT RAQ $183.
- The Competitor EXM catch-all ad group (50 of 58 enrollments, $363 CPA) was replaced by per-competitor ad groups in the week of Sep 14. Two weeks in: $3.65k spend, 6 enrollments, too early to judge.
- Demand Gen: 128.8k clicks at $0.08 CPC and a 0.07% conversion rate, so the traffic is almost entirely low quality. Spend was cut to about $300/week from Aug 31.
- Brand state ad groups perform about the same as Brand_General ($153–184 vs $167–175 CPA).
