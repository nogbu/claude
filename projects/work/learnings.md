# Learnings: work (Google Ads and Bing Ads SEM)

Settled facts for the work project, one per line, with where each came from and when it was confirmed.

- Google Ads date range: the date range is a saved viewing preference that "affects your entire account", stored for each user, and in a manager account it's synced to every account under the manager you're signed in to ("Your preferences are stored in the manager account where you're signed in") (source: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9758020, confirmed 2026-11-05)
- Google Ads date range pages: Google's help doesn't say whether pages like Change history, saved reports or Insights keep their own date range (source: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9758020 and https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2454008, checked 2026-11-05)
